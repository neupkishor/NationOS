# **App Name**: NationOS: Unified Governance

## Core Features:

- Citizen Registry: Manage citizen data: births, deaths, family, residency, and migration.
- Economic & Employment Tracking: Track household income, employment, skills, and eligibility for loans and subsidies.
- Agriculture & Livestock Module: Monitor crop production, sales, and livestock counts for food security and policy.
- Residency & Migration Tracker: Visualize internal and international migration patterns.
- Infrastructure & Resources: Track roads, schools, hospitals, water supply, and utilities.
- Smart Reporting Engine: Generate custom reports at various levels: ward, municipality, district, province, with visualizations. This tool reasons when or whether to incorporate various pieces of info into the report

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep blue (#3F51B5) for trustworthiness and stability, reflecting government reliability.
- Background color: Light blue-gray (#E8EAF6) for a clean and professional feel.
- Accent color: Complementary amber (#FFB300) to highlight key metrics and CTAs.
- Body and headline font: 'Inter' for a modern and objective feel.
- Use clear, consistent icons to represent data categories.
- Prioritize a clear and logical layout for complex datasets.
- Subtle transitions to enhance the user experience when navigating reports.
